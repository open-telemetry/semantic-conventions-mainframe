Rename `zos.software` entity identity attribute from `mainframe.lpar.name` to `mainframe.partition.name`.

The `zos.software` entity previously used `mainframe.lpar.name` as part of its composite identity (alongside `zos.sysplex.name` and `zos.smf.id`). This attribute has been removed and replaced by `mainframe.partition.name`, which is the canonical identity attribute of the `mainframe.partition` entity refinement and applies uniformly to both Classic-mode LPARs and DPM-mode partitions.

Existing z/OS emitters that set `mainframe.lpar.name` on `zos.software` resource attributes must rename the attribute to `mainframe.partition.name` to conform to this definition.
